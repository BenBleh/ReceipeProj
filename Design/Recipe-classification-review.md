## Entries worth checking

- **Aperol spritz**, **bechamel** and **pickle liquid:** now Other as a drink,
  standalone sauce and pickling preparation, respectively.
- **test:** placeholder ingredients and a nonsensical method do not establish
  a dish, so proposed as Other. This does not make it a valid recipe; decide
  whether the test entry should remain. It has not been removed.
- **pesto pasta salad:** ingredients and method both say "see link". Sides is
  a tentative title-based proposal; the saved text cannot establish whether
  this is intended as a complete meal. Other is not used merely because a
  recognisable dish has incomplete instructions.
- **El pastor:** remains Mains. Although the ingredient list describes a
  marinade, the method also marinates and cooks meat; it is not just a sauce.
- **FETA PASTA SALAD** and **Loaded Greek Potatoes:** proposed as Mains because
  their recipes include chicken and assemble a complete dish.
- **Potato, Bean & Egg Salad**, **Roasted Sweet Potato & Chickpea Salad with
  Yogurt** and **PUFF PASTRY MUSHROOM GRUYERE TART:** proposed as vegetarian
  Mains. They could be Sides if that is how you normally serve them.
- **High Protein Corn Fritters!**, **English Muffins** and the other savoury
  breads: proposed as Sides rather than introducing breakfast/snack categories.
